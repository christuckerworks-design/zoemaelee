Black-owned verification

Two tiers. Self-identified is instant. Verified uses a third-party certifier — we never judge who is or isn't Black ourselves.

★
Self-identified ★ Black-ownedYou check a box. Instant, free, no documents. Shown as self-identified.
✓
Verified ✓ VerifiedConfirmed by an independent certifier or business documents. Higher trust, unlocks the ✓ badge.

How to get verified

📜
ByBlack CertificationIssued by the U.S. Black Chambers. The standard for Black-owned businesses.
🏛️
NMSDC / MBE CertificationNational Minority Supplier Development Council — 51%+ ownership documentation.
🏘️
Local Black ChamberMembership or certification from a regional Black chamber of commerce.
📄
Business documentsEntity registration + ownership attestation, reviewed manually.
🔒 Zoemaelee never determines who is Black. It displays either your self-attestation or a third party's certification. No one is denied access for being unverified.
